• Kyle Schumacher-Picone
  • About

Kyle is a designer and writer based in New York. He has practiced design since childhood and has had the opportunity to work with multiple global brands.

His approach to design focuses on putting craft first in the effort to let the pure aesthetics of design wow the viewer. Beyond design, he is a multi-faceted communications expert having served in a variety of roles as editor, organizer, and people-herder.

As his own previous sites can attest to, the most valuable thing he has to offer is himself: if you can’t be kind, if you can’t be personable, then what’s the point? We’re all in this together. xo—k.
